The Best Tunes You Never Play

by Aaron O'Rourke

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-20-8 · Download one-sheet (PDF)

The Best Tunes You Never Play collects 30 fiddle tunes arranged for mountain dulcimer by Aaron O'Rourke, all of them tunes that rarely come up in dulcimer circles.

Most are fairly simple to play. For the more complex ones, Aaron adds a simplified version for beginners.

Free downloadable audio demonstrates the tunes. Get the audio files from Folkcraft.

54 pages.
