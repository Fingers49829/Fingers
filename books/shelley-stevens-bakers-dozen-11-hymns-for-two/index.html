Baker's Dozen #11: Hymns For Two offers 13 hymns arranged as mountain dulcimer duets by award-winning arranger Shelley Stevens.

All the duets are in DAD tuning, with standard notation and tablature for both parts.

19 pages.

Songs included:

All Hail The Power - DAD Tuning - Composed By E. Perronet, J. Rippon, And O. Holden
Blessed Assurance - DAD Tuning - Composed By F. Crosby And J. Knapp
Faith Of Our Fathers - DAD Tuning - Composed By F. Faber And H. Hemy
God Be With You - DAD Tuning - Composed By J. Rankin And W. Tomer
Holy, Holy, Holy - DAD Tuning - Composed By R. Heber And J. Dykes
Jesus Loves Me - DAD Tuning - Composed By William Bradbury
Nearer My God To Thee - DAD Tuning - Composed By S. Adams And L. Mason
Near The Cross - DAD Tuning - Composed By F. Crosby And W. Doane
Precious Memories - DAD Tuning - Composed By J. Wright
Rock Of Ages - DAD Tuning - Composed By A. Toplady And T. Hastings
Softly And Tenderly - DAD Tuning - Composed By W. Thompson
Sweet Hour Of Prayer - DAD Tuning - Composed By W. Walford And W. Bradbury
Take Time To Be Holy - DAD Tuning - Composed By W. Longstaff And G. Stebbins
