Simply Christmas

by Maureen Sellers

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-66-6

Maureen Sellers has written an entire series of books in her "Simply" style. With unadorned melodies, beautiful harmonies, and easy-to-read tablature, the books are exceptional.

This book, "Simply Christmas", is no different - it is utterly amazing, and has been one of our more popular books for over a decade. It is full of great dulcimer tablature, from a master arranger. You'll like it.
