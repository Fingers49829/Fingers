Simply Christmas is Maureen Sellers' Christmas collection for mountain dulcimer, and one of the most popular books in her "Simply" series for more than a decade.

Like every book in the series, it pairs unadorned melodies with beautiful harmonies in easy-to-read tablature.

55 pages.
