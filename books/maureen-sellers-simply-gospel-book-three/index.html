by Maureen Sellers

Simply Gospel, Book Three brings old-time gospel songs, hymns and spirituals to the mountain dulcimer. It's part of Maureen Sellers' "Simply" series, written for novice to intermediate players.

  • All songs in DAD tuning; some use a capo
  • Standard notation and tablature, with lyrics and chords
  • Author's notes on every song

50 pages.
