Simply Gospel, Book Two

by Maureen Sellers

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-71-0

Simply Gospel, Book Two follows Maureen Sellers' best-selling Simply Gospel, Book One with more gospel songs arranged for solo mountain dulcimer.

It ranges widely, from children's songs to traditional old-time gospel. Lyrics are included, and a few tunes use a capo.

58 pages.
