Richard Ash's Fret Position Reference Guide For Ukulele, Guitar, Banjo, Mandolin, And Other Chromatic Fretted Instruments solves one of the trickiest parts of building a fretted instrument: where the frets go.

Detailed fret placement charts cover every scale length from 8 to 32 inches, in eighth-inch steps, so you can lay out an accurate fingerboard for your next guitar, ukulele or other string instrument.

It's not a book to read cover to cover, but one to keep in the shop and use again and again. The compiler, Richard Ash, is the owner and a luthier at Folkcraft Instruments.
