Baker's Dozen #13: Patriotic

by Shelley Stevens

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-30-7

Baker's Dozen #13: Patriotic packs 15 patriotic arrangements for mountain dulcimer by Shelley Stevens (two more than a baker's dozen!).

  • Service songs: Army, Navy, Air Force and more
  • Classic patriotic songs by Irving Berlin, George M. Cohan and others
  • Traditional American favorites such as "America" and "Battle Hymn Of The Republic"

The arrangements are very accessible, all in DAD tuning with no capo. Five of them use the 1+ fret.

25 pages.

Songs included:

America (My Country 'Tis Of Thee) - DAD Tuning - Composed By Samuel F. Smith
America The Beautiful - DAD Tuning - Composed By Katherine Lee Bates And Samuel Augustus Ward
Anchors Aweigh - DAD Tuning - Composed By Charles A. Zimmermann And Alfred Hart Miles
Battle Hymn Of The Republic - DAD Tuning - Composed By Julia Ward Howe And William Steffe
Hail! Columbia - DAD Tuning - Composed By Joseph Hopkinson And Philip Phile
Lord Guard And Guide The Men Who Fly - DAD Tuning - Composed By Mary Hamilton And Henry Baker
Marine's Hymn - DAD Tuning - Composed By Jacques Offenbach, Lyricist Unknown
Over There - DAD Tuning - Composed By George M. Cohan
Semper Paratus - DAD Tuning - Composed By Captain Frances S. Van Boskerk
Star Spangled Banner - DAD Tuning - Lyrics By Francis Scott Key
Stars And Stripes Forever - DAD Tuning - Composed By John Philip Sousa
The Caissons Go Rolling Along - DAD Tuning - Composed By Edmund L. Gruber
When Johnny Comes Marching Home - DAD Tuning - Composed By Patrick S. Gilmore
Yankee Doodle Boy - DAD Tuning - Composed By George M. Cohan
You're A Grand Old Flag - DAD Tuning - Composed By George M. Cohan
