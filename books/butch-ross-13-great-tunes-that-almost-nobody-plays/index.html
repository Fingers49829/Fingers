13 Great Tunes That (Almost) Nobody Plays

by Butch Ross

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-94-9

Bored with the same old dulcimer songs? 13 Great Tunes That (Almost) Nobody Plays is a mountain dulcimer tab book from Butch Ross full of great melodies that aren't the usual standards.

Some are fresh tunes and some are old tunes done in a new way, all with Butch's knack for pushing the edges. Expect your friends to ask you to keep playing.

31 pages.
