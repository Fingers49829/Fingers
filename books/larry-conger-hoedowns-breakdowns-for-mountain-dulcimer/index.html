Hoedowns & Breakdowns For Mountain Dulcimer

by Larry Conger

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

You can't have a dance (hoedown or otherwise) without some great music. You won't find any crooked tunes here (well, not many) - you'll find great melodies, danceable rhythms, and a ton of fun. You NEED this book in your dulcimer music library.
