The Classical Dulcimer

by Larry Conger

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

The Classical Dulcimer brings nearly 30 classical pieces to the mountain dulcimer, arranged by Larry Conger.

Most of this music predates the dulcimer itself, and it has stood the test of time. It's a lot of fun to play.

46 pages.
