Lullabies

by Dana Gruber

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-48-2

This book of dulcimer lullaby sheet music contains a nice mix of solo, duet, and ensemble versions of the most popular (and beautiful) lullabies from the history of mankind. Arranged by the ever-talented Dana Gruber, this collection of dulcimer tab (with standard notation and lyrics, too!) has something for each of your "lullaby moods" - classical beauties to folk tunes, and everything in between.

52 pages.

This book is published by Folkcraft® Instruments, under the Fingers Of Steel® imprint.
