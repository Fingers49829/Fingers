A Long Way From Shady Grove

by Butch Ross

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-89-5

Butch Ross has a unique style when it comes to dulcimer music that is totally his own, and his playing leaves jaws dropped everywhere he goes. "A Long Way From Shady Grove" is a sheet music and tablature transcription from his vastly popular CD release of the same name.

From older songs with a modern spin to some originals that encompass Butch's personality, this book will leave you wanting more while helping you take the dulcimer into places it has never been, just like Butch does!
