A Long Way From Shady Grove

by Butch Ross

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-89-5 · Download one-sheet (PDF)

A Long Way From Shady Grove is the companion songbook to Butch Ross's popular CD of the same name, with sheet music and mountain dulcimer tablature transcribed from the recording.

It mixes older songs given a modern spin with Butch originals, in the distinctive style that takes the dulcimer into new places.

87 pages.
