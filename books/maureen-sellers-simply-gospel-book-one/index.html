Simply Gospel, Book One

by Maureen Sellers

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-70-3 · Download one-sheet (PDF)

Simply Gospel, Book One collects gospel tunes Maureen Sellers has gathered through many years of playing, teaching and performing on the mountain dulcimer.

The arrangements are easy, and they make a good starting point for creating your own versions of each song.

53 pages.
