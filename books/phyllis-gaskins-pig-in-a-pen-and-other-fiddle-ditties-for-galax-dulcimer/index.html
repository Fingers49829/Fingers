Pig In A Pen (And Other Fiddle Ditties For Galax Dulcimer)

by Phyllis Gaskins

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-57-4

"Pig In A Pen" follows Phyllis's first book, "A Job Of Journey Work", and is also a comprehensive, informative book dedicated to the unique playing and construction style of the Galax Dulcimer. Included are songs with playing instructions and Phyllis' own adapted notation showing the authentic playing style of the Galax dulcimer. History and construction as well as some of the artists who defined this style are also included.

Phyllis has illustrated this book around the title theme. Her magic as an artist and illustrator abounds as the book is not only informative but beautiful.

All songs in this book assume that all strings are tuned to D. Songs noted below as D tunes have the "Do" starting at fret zero. Songs noted below as G tunes have the "Do" starting at fret three. Songs noted below as A tunes have the "Do" starting at fret four, and recommend that you use a partial capo (drone strings only) at fret one. Songs noted below as E minor tunes have the "Do" starting at fret one, and suggest either tuning the drone strings to E's, or using a partial capo at fret one. (Phyllis talks about how to make a partial capo - false nut - on page nineteen of her book "A Job Of Journey Work".)

Instructions are also included for playing these songs in DAD tuning.
