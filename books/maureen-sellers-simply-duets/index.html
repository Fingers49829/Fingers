Simply Duets

by Maureen Sellers

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-67-3 · Download one-sheet (PDF)

Simply Duets is a large collection of older songs arranged for mountain dulcimer duet by educator, performer and author Maureen Sellers.

The arrangements strike a happy middle ground: not too hard, but not elementary either.

119 pages.
