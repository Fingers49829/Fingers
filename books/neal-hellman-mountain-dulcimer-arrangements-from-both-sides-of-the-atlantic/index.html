Mountain Dulcimer Arrangements From Both Sides Of The Atlantic

by Neal Hellman

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-13-0

With 35 arrangements from "Both Sides Of The Atlantic", Neal Hellman's latest book is the RIGHT combination of everything you want in a dulcimer book. Challenging arrangements (not JUST melody lines!), but not TOO challenging. Songs you've heard of, plus some that are new to you. Songs with lyrics PLUS instrumental tunes. This is a great book from one of the masters of the mountain dulcimer.

Free downloads of demo tracks can be found here.

This book is published by Folkcraft® Instruments, under the Fingers Of Steel® imprint.

84 pages.
