Subtitled "A unique collection of carols," Larry Conger's Christmas With The Dulcimer offers superb mountain dulcimer arrangements of a wide variety of Christmas carols.

It's fun to play, and it makes a great Christmas gift for dulcimer-playing friends and family.

32 pages.
