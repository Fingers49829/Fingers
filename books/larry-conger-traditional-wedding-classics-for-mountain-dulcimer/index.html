Traditional Wedding Classics For Mountain Dulcimer is Larry Conger's collection of classic wedding tunes arranged for solo mountain dulcimer.

When a friend asks you to play at their wedding, this book has what you need, and the tunes are fun to play any day.

20 pages.

Songs included:

Air - Handel - DAD Tuning
Bridal Chorus - Wagner - DAD Tuning
Canon In D - Pachelbel - DAD Tuning 
Jesu, Joy Of Man's Desiring - Bach - DAD Tuning
Morning Has Broken - Traditional Gaelic - DAD Tuning
Ode To Joy - Beethoven - DAD Tuning
Processional - Handel - DAD Tuning
Trumpet Voluntary - Clarke - DAD Tuning
Wedding March - Mendelssohn - DAD Tuning
