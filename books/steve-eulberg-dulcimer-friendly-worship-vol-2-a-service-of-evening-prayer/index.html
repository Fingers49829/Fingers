Dulcimer-Friendly Worship, Vol. 2: A Service Of Evening Prayer

by Steve Eulberg

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-74-1

Steve Eulberg's "Dulcimer-Friendly Worship, Vol. 2" is a gentle, contemplative service of Evening Prayer with traditional hymns and original psalm settings with mountain dulcimer tablature for both DAA and DAD tunings.

The goal of Steve Eulberg's "Dulcimer-Friendly Worship" series is to make the hymns and carols of the church available and accessible to dulcimer players. Accompaniment CD included!
