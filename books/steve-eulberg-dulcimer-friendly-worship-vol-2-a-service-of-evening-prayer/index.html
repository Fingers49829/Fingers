Dulcimer-Friendly Worship, Vol. 2: A Service Of Evening Prayer by Steve Eulberg is a gentle, contemplative Evening Prayer service with traditional hymns and original psalm settings.

Mountain dulcimer tablature is given for both DAA and DAD tunings, and an accompaniment CD is included. Part of a series that makes the church's hymns and carols accessible to dulcimer players.

37 pages.
