Baker's Dozen #10: Ragtime And Honky Tonk

by Shelley Stevens

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-62-8

This fun, quirky book of sheet music features 13 classic American ragtime tunes arranged by Shelley Stevens. You'll be playing favorites by Scott Joplin and Hughie Cannon on your mountain dulcimer, and impressing your friends with your ragtime and honky tonk skills!

All arrangements include both standard notation and dulcimer tablature.

This book is published by Folkcraft® Instruments, under the Fingers Of Steel® imprint.

20 pages.

Songs included:

Alabama Jubilee - DAD Tuning - Composed by: George Cobb And Jack Yellen
Alexander’s Ragtime Band - DAD Tuning - Composed by: Irving Berlin
Bill Bailey, Won't You Please Come Home? - DAD Tuning - Composed by: Hughie Cannon
Darktown Strutter's Ball - DAD Tuning - Composed by: Shelton Brooks
Dill Pickle Rag - DAD Tuning - Composed by: Chas. E. Johnson
Down Home Rag - DAD Tuning - Composed by: Wilbur C. Sweatman
Down Yonder - DAD Tuning - Composed by: L. Wolfe Gilbert
Dulcimer Rag - DAD Tuning - Composed by: Shelley Stevens
Hello Ma Baby - DAD Tuning - Composed by: Joseph E. Howard And Ida Emerson
Hot Time In The Old Town - DAD Tuning - Composed by: Joe Hayden And Theo A. Metz
Peacherine Rag - DAD Tuning - Composed by: Scott Joplin
Ragtime Cowboy Joe - DAD Tuning - Composed by: Grant Clarke, Lewis F. Muir, And Maurice Abrahams
Waitin’ For The Robert E. Lee - DAD Tuning - Composed by: Lewis F. Muir And L. Wolfe Gilbert
