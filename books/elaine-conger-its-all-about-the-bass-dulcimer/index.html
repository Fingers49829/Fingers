It's All About The Bass Dulcimer

by Elaine Conger

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

Download one-sheet (PDF)

It's All About The Bass Dulcimer by Elaine Conger is a bass dulcimer companion to 100 jam favorites, the tunes you'll hear again and again at jams, each with a bass line pattern. Bring it to jam sessions, or use it to add a bass part to an arrangement you already play.

The tab is in DAD tuning and sticks close to what a bass player would play. Expect single-note accompaniment parts, not solo arrangements.

56 pages.
