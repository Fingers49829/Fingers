Baker's Dozen #12: Jam Tunes And Beyond is an intermediate-level mountain dulcimer book for players ready for more than simple arrangements.

Shelley Stevens arranges jam classics such as "Mississippi Sawyer" and "June Apple," plus a few tunes that may be new to you. Each includes standard notation and dulcimer tablature.

18 pages.

Songs included:

The Ashgrove - DAD Tuning - Welsh Aire
Billy In The Low Ground - DAD Tuning
