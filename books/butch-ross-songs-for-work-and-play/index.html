Songs For Work And Play

by Butch Ross

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-90-1

Butch Ross's Songs For Work And Play gives you great traditional songs for mountain dulcimer in two versions each: an easy arrangement and a more challenging one.

Beginners can start with the easy versions and move to the harder arrangements as their skills grow. Advanced players can jump straight into the challenging ones and play with Butch's technical flair and expressive style.

79 pages.
