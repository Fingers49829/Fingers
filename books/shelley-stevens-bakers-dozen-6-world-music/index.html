Baker's Dozen #6: World Music

by Shelley Stevens

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-29-1

Baker's Dozen #6: World Music takes your mountain dulcimer around the globe, with songs from eight countries from Europe to the Americas, arranged by Shelley Stevens.

Standard notation, tablature and lyrics are included. All songs are in DAD tuning; several use a capo, as noted in the song list below.

19 pages.

Songs included:

Ar Hyd T Nos (All Through The Night) - DAD Tuning - Wales
Au Clair De La Lune - DAD Tuning - France
Aupres De Ma Blonde - DAD Tuning - France
Du, D Liegst Mir Im Herzen - DAD Tuning - Germany
Ei, Ukhnem (Song Of The Volga Boatmen) - DAD Tuning, Capo 1 - Russia
El Coqui (The Frog) - DAD Tuning - Puerto Rico
Flow Gently Sweet Afton - DAD Tuning - Scotland
Folkje Vinta Hume - DAD Tuning - Norway
Guantanamera - DAD Tuning - Cuba
Round-Dance - DAD Tuning, Capo 1 - Russia
Santa Lucia - DAD Tuning - Italy
Scarborough Fair - DAD Tuning, Capo 1 - England
Wee Croodin’ Doo - DAD Tuning, Capo 1 - Scotland
