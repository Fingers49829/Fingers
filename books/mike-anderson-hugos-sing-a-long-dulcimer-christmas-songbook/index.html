Hugo's Sing-A-Long Dulcimer Christmas Songbook

by Mike Anderson

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-40-6

Unique children's Christmas songs - this songbook is a must-have for Christmas music fans and dulcimer players! Mike Anderson has arranged all the classic and beloved Christmas songs, plus an array of unique children's songs for any instrument that can play chords. All songs are presented in lead sheet format with lyrics and chord symbols. Make your Christmas season memorable with Mike Anderson's "Hugo's Sing-A-Long Dulcimer Christmas Songbook".
