Hugo's Sing-A-Long Dulcimer Christmas Songbook

by Mike Anderson

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-40-6 · Download one-sheet (PDF)

Hugo's Sing-A-Long Dulcimer Christmas Songbook by Mike Anderson combines classic, beloved Christmas songs with an array of unique children's Christmas songs.

Every song is written in lead sheet format with lyrics and chord symbols, so you can play it on dulcimer or any instrument that plays chords.

Songs included:

For Auld Lang Syne - Robert Burns - DAD Tuning
Away In A Manger - DAD Tuning
The Big Red Sleigh - Music and words by Mike Anderson and Hugo Kringle - DAD Tuning
Deck The Halls - DAD Tuning
Eight Little Reindeer - Music and words by Mike Anderson - DAD Tuning
Here We Come A-Caroling - Traditional - DAD Tuning
Jingle Bells - J. Pierpont, 1857 - DAD Tuning
Jolly Old St. Nicholas - J. Pierpont & B. Hanby, 1860s - DAD Tuning
Joy To The World - DAD Tuning
Over The River & Through The Wood - Lydia Maria Child, 1844 - DAD Tuning
Silent Night - Franz Xaver Gruber, 1818 - DAD Tuning (CGC tuning suggested for singing)
This Christmas - Music and lyrics by Mike Anderson - DAD Tuning
Up On The Housetop - B. Hanby, 1864 - DAD Tuning
