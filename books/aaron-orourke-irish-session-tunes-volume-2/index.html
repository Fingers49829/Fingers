Irish Session Tunes, Volume 2

by Aaron O'Rourke

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-17-8

Aaron O'Rourke's Irish Session Tunes, Volume 2 adds more jigs, reels and polkas commonly played at Irish sessions, all arranged for mountain dulcimer.

Like Volume 1, it opens with a brief guide to playing in an Irish session and explains the ornamentation used in Irish music.
