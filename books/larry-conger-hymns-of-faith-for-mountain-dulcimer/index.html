Hymns Of Faith For Mountain Dulcimer features Larry Conger's mountain dulcimer arrangements of beloved hymns.

You'll find famous classics such as "Abide With Me" and "Just As I Am" alongside lesser-known hymns like "O Worship The King" and "Sunshine In My Soul."

32 pages.
