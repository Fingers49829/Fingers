I Celebrate Life!

by Steve Eulberg

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-78-9 · Download one-sheet (PDF)

I Celebrate Life! is a book/CD set of original songs and tunes for mountain dulcimer and hammered dulcimer, spanning the first 25 years of Steve Eulberg's music.

It moves from a boot-kicking hoedown through relaxing interludes to jigs and waltzes.

84 pages.
