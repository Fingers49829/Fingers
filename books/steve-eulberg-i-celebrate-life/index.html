I Celebrate Life!

by Steve Eulberg

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-78-9

A boot-kicking hoe-down with relaxing, breath-catching interludes, followed by jig steps and waltzes, "I Celebrate Life!" is a collection of original songs and tunes for both the mountain dulcimer and hammered dulcimer. This book/CD set spans the first 25 years of Steve Eulberg's music.
