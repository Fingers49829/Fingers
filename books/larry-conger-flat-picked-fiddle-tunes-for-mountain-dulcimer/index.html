Flat-Picked Fiddle Tunes For Mountain Dulcimer

by Larry Conger

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

Tired of the same few fiddle tunes? Flat-Picked Fiddle Tunes For Mountain Dulcimer by Larry Conger is full of fiddle tunes most players don't know yet.

Larry's arrangements are excellent, and the tunes are a lot of fun to learn.

36 pages.
