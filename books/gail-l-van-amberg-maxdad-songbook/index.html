MaxDAD Songbook

by Gail L. Van Amberg

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-80-2 · Download one-sheet (PDF)

Gail L. Van Amberg wrote this songbook for players of the Folkcraft® MaxDAD® mountain dulcimer, the five-string dulcimer tuned DADAD. It brings together 33 favorite tunes, from Irish and Scottish airs and Welsh hymns to classical melodies and an American cowboy song, each arranged by Gail with standard notation, chords and MaxDAD tab. Two different arrangements of The Ashgrove are included.

All of the tab is written for a standard MaxDAD with a 1+ and a 6+ fret. Two-page arrangements are laid out to open flat across facing pages, so there are no page turns in the middle of a tune.

This book is published by Fingers Of Steel®.

76 pages.
