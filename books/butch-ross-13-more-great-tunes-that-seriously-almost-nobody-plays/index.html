13 More Great Tunes That Seriously (Almost) Nobody Plays is Butch Ross's second collection of rarely heard tunes for mountain dulcimer. Some are a bit challenging; most are simply obscure, and well worth knowing.

Almost every tune is in the key of D, in DAD tuning. When a tune is usually played in a different key, the book also includes a version in its traditional key.

37 pages.
