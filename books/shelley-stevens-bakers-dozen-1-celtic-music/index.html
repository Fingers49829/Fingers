Baker's Dozen #1: Celtic Music is the first volume of Shelley Stevens' Baker's Dozen series: 13 well-known Celtic melodies arranged for solo mountain dulcimer.

Every tune has standard notation and 3-line tablature in DAD tuning, and the chord melody arrangements will help you develop your playing.

21 pages.
