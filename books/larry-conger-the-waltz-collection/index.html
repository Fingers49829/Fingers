The Waltz Collection

by Larry Conger

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 979-8-953855-12-9 · Download one-sheet (PDF)

Fifteen beautiful waltzes from Larry Conger, all in DAD tuning. Most are traditional American fiddle and dance tunes in the "country" waltz style, played with that lovely shuffling, lilting rhythm - plus the old parlor song "I'll Be All Smiles Tonight" and Larry's own "Western Carolina Waltz". Along the way, Larry includes short notes on the history of the waltz and tips on slides, hammer-ons, pull-offs, triplets and pinching. A recording of every tune is included, so you can hear exactly how each one should sound. Intermediate level.
