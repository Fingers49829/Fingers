Baker's Dozen #9: My First Dulcimer Book

by Shelley Stevens

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-24-6

Baker's Dozen #9: My First Dulcimer Book is Shelley Stevens' mountain dulcimer book for young hands, with twelve familiar tunes instead of the series' usual thirteen.

Nearly every song comes in two versions: a one-string version played on the melody string alone, and a two-string version that adds the middle string. Children can get comfortable on the dulcimer step by step.

22 pages.

Songs included:
