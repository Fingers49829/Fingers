Ready to move past playing everything on open strings? Beyond D: 18 Tunes In "A" And In "G" is Jim Miller's mountain dulcimer collection for learning to play with a capo in the keys of A and G.

From one of the dulcimer's most respected instructors, the tunes are carefully chosen to build your repertoire, keep it fun, and make you comfortable using a capo. Every tune is written in standard notation and tab, and the book includes Jim's lists of common jam tunes in A and G.

30 pages.
