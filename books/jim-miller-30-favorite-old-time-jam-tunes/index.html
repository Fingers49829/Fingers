30 Favorite Old Time Jam Tunes

by Jim Miller

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com

Performer and educator Jim Miller arranges 30 old-time jam tunes for mountain dulcimer, some traditional and some contemporary, in 30 Favorite Old Time Jam Tunes.

Expect to recognize plenty of them and to discover a few you haven't played before.

15 pages.
