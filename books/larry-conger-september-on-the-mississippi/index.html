September On The Mississippi

by Larry Conger

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

"September On The Mississippi" is Larry Conger's signature song - you hear it all the time, and enjoy it every time you hear it.

This superb collection of arrangements for mountain dulcimer includes "September", plus 19 other great tunes for your enjoyment.

Some capo is required for this book, and songs are mostly in DAD tuning, with one song in DAA and one song in DGD.

32 pages. Songs included:
