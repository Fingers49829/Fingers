September On The Mississippi

by Larry Conger

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

Download one-sheet (PDF)

September On The Mississippi is named for Larry Conger's signature tune, a favorite every time it's played. This mountain dulcimer collection includes "September" plus 19 more of Larry's arrangements.

Most songs are in DAD tuning, with one in DAA and one in DGD. Some songs use a capo.

32 pages.
