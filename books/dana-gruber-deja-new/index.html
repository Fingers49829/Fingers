Deja-New

by Dana Gruber

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-44-4

Deja-New by Dana Gruber puts a new twist on classic and traditional songs. The arrangements are written for mountain dulcimer, with optional guitar chords so you can play them with a guitarist.

Fresh, inventive takes on familiar music make this a collection you'll come back to again and again.

51 pages.
