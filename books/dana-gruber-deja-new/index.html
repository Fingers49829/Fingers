Deja-New

by Dana Gruber

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-44-4

Take a musical journey with Dana Gruber's latest collection "Deja-New," a blissful selection of mountain dulcimer arrangements with guitar chords. A fantastic fusion of sound, this book is packed with fresh and exciting arrangements that will take your breath away. Take a break from the ordinary and immerse yourself in a musical experience you'll be deja-vu-ing over and over again!

A collection of classic and traditional songs - with a new twist. For mountain dulcimer with optional guitar chords.

51 pages.
