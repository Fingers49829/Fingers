Standard And Baritone Dulcimer Duets

by Larry Conger

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

Duets for standard (DAD) and baritone (AEA) dulcimers? Sure - with this book, you'll be all set! The standard dulcimer uses DAD and DGD tunings, and often uses the 1.5 fret. Some capo use is also required.

With your purchase, you'll get TWO books (one for each player).

46 pages, each book (92 pages total).
