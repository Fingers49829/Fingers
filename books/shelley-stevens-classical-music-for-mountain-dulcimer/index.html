Classical Music For Mountain Dulcimer

by Shelley Stevens

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-05-5

This book has twenty-six classical tunes arranged for solo mountain dulcimer. These intermediate arrangements include both standard notation and tablature, and are written for a DAD-tuned dulcimer.

All of the arrangements require the 6.5 fret, many require the 1.5 fret, and a few require a 0.5 fret. All songs are in DAD tuning.

This collection was originally published under the name "The Three String Orchestra, Vol. II", and was later renamed "Classical Music For Mountain Dulcimer".

37 pages.
