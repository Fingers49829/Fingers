Classical Music For Mountain Dulcimer

by Shelley Stevens

Classical Music For Mountain Dulcimer by Shelley Stevens contains 26 classical pieces arranged for solo mountain dulcimer in DAD tuning, at intermediate level, with standard notation and tablature.

Every arrangement uses the 6.5 fret, many use the 1.5 fret, and a few need a 0.5 fret.

37 pages. Originally published as The Three String Orchestra, Vol. II.
