Irish Session Tunes, Volume 1

by Aaron O'Rourke

"Irish Session Tunes, Volume 1" is a collection of 36 jigs, reels, and polkas commonly played in Irish sessions arranged for the mountain dulcimer. Aaron O'Rourke includes a brief introduction to playing in an Irish session and explanation of Irish ornamentation.

This amazing book of dulcimer songs includes free downloadable audio files that you can use to teach your ear how the tunes and exercises should sound. Here's a link to the page with our free audio downloads.
