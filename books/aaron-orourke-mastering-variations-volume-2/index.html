Mastering Variations, Volume 2 gives you 7 more common dulcimer club and festival jam tunes, each with rhythmic, melodic and harmonic variations arranged for mountain dulcimer by Aaron O'Rourke.

As in Volume 1, every tune comes in four versions (Rhythmic, Melodic, Harmonic and All-Put-Together), plus a basic version to help you learn it first.

Free downloadable audio demonstrates the variations. Get the audio files from Folkcraft.

57 pages.
