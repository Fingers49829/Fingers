Sunday Songs For Kids by Mike Anderson is a songbook of Christian children's songs. The chords are included, so you can play them on dulcimer or on guitar, banjo or anything else that plays chords.

Each song appears two ways:

  • In standard notation with the first verse
  • With every verse and the chords, so it's easy to play along

73 pages.
