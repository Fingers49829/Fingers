Songs Of The Civil War

by Maureen Sellers

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-68-0 · Download one-sheet (PDF)

In Songs Of The Civil War, music historian Maureen Sellers selects songs that were popular during the American Civil War and arranges them for Appalachian dulcimer.

The mountain dulcimer was only about 60 years old at the time, and these songs still translate beautifully to the instrument today.

22 pages.
