Jamming With The Baritone Dulcimer In The Key Of D

by Shelley Stevens

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-11-6

This book will show you that just by retuning one string you can easily play in the key of D and not only join the jam but enhance it by adding a low voice to the mix.

Thirty-six tunes in ADA tuning with standard notation, chords and three line tablature.

The downloadable audio files (with all 36 tunes) are available for free on this page.

This book is published by Folkcraft® Instruments, under the Fingers Of Steel® imprint.
