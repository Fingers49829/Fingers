by Shelley Stevens

Jamming With The Baritone Dulcimer In The Key Of D by Shelley Stevens shows how retuning just one string lets your baritone dulcimer play easily in D, so you can join the jam and add a low voice to it.

The book has 36 tunes in ADA tuning, with standard notation, chords and 3-line tablature. Audio of all 36 tunes is a free download. Get the audio files from Folkcraft.
