Using The 1 1/2 Fret In DAD Tuning

by Shelley Stevens

Printed bookBuy at Folkcraft.com PDF downloadBuy at DulcimerDownloads.com

ISBN 978-1-964725-10-9

Unlock the mystery of  what to do with your 1 ½ (also written 1.5 and 1*) fret. With this book, you'll learn chords, scales and tunes that use the fret. Shelley has included lots of songs that use the 1.5 fret, and will help you get comfortable with its use, and use the extra fret to the fullest potential. You'll learn to play easily in the keys of  G,  C and D Minor.

The downloadable audio files include 30 MP3 tracks, demonstrating most of the tunes in this book. You can download the zipped folder of audio files from this page.

This book is published by Folkcraft® Instruments, under the Fingers Of Steel® imprint.
