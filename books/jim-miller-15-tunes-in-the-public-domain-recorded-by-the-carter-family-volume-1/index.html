Jim Miller arranges 15 traditional, public-domain songs recorded by the Carter Family for solo mountain dulcimer in 15 Tunes In The Public Domain Recorded By The Carter Family, Volume 1.

Each song includes dulcimer tablature along with lyrics and chord symbols.

33 pages.
