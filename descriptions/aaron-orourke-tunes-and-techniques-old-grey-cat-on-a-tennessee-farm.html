"Old Grey Cat On A Tennessee Farm" is the core tune of this book in Aaron O'Rourke's five-part Tunes And Techniques series for mountain dulcimer.

Rather than just handing you the tab, Aaron uses the old-time tune to teach techniques, from fundamentals to a few advanced moves, so the lessons stick because they're tied to real music.

Audio samples are available as a free download. Get the audio files from Folkcraft.
