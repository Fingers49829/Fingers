Not sure what to do with the 1 1/2 fret (also written 1.5 or 1*) on your dulcimer? Shelley Stevens' Using The 1 1/2 Fret In DAD Tuning teaches the chords, scales and tunes that use it.

Lots of songs help you get comfortable with the extra fret and make the most of it, and it opens up the keys of G, C and D minor so you can play in them without strain.

Most of the tunes are demonstrated on 30 free MP3 tracks. Get the audio files from Folkcraft.
