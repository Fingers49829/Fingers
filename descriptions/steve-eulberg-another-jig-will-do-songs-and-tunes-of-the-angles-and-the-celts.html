Steve Eulberg's Another Jig Will Do: Songs And Tunes Of The Angles And The Celts gathers Irish, English and Scottish music in arrangements for both mountain dulcimer and hammered dulcimer.

The mix includes jigs and slip jigs, reels, hornpipes, slides, strathspeys, waltzes, airs and ballads. Mountain dulcimer arrangements use DAA and DAd tunings, with some capo use.

74 pages.
