Baritone And Soprano Duets For Mountain Dulcimer by Shelley Stevens has 26 duets for a DAD-tuned standard dulcimer and a baritone dulcimer tuned either ADA or AEA.

Drawing on Shelley's long experience as a teacher and performer, the book explores the sound of both baritone tunings. Standard notation and dulcimer tablature are included.

42 pages.
