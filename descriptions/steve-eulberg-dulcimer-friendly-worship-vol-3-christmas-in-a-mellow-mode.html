Steve Eulberg's third Dulcimer-Friendly Worship book, Christmas In A Mellow Mode, collects 32 minor-key tunes from around the world. Each has mountain dulcimer tab, standard notation, and chord shapes for portable hammered dulcimer.

The tunes come from Christmas traditions in many cultures, but they suit the whole winter season. Styles range from jazz waltzes and shape-note hymns to European dances, African-American spirituals and blues.

76 pages.
