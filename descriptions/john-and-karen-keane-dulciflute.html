DulciFlute is a book of duets for mountain dulcimer and Native American flute by John and Karen Keane. It features 16 original tunes, from "Caddo Lake Waltz," which John and Karen often play live, to "The Leaves Of Shongaloo."

The dulcimer is tuned DAD and the flute is in B minor. An audio CD with a full recording of every tune is included, so you can listen or play along.

27 pages.
