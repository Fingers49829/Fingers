Aaron O'Rourke's Tunes And Techniques series teaches mountain dulcimer skills one tune at a time. In Tunes And Techniques – Liza Jane, the old-time tune "Liza Jane" is the vehicle.

As you learn the tune, Aaron introduces fundamental dulcimer techniques and some more advanced ones. It's a compact way to study with a top teacher and have fun doing it. The series has five books in all.

Free downloadable audio samples go with the book. Get the audio files from Folkcraft.
