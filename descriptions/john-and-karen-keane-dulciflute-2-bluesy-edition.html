DulciFlute 2 (Bluesy Edition) is the follow-up to John and Karen Keane's DulciFlute, with more of their original duets pairing mountain dulcimer and Native American flute.

The dulcimer parts are in DAD tuning and the flute parts are for a D minor flute, and a conversion/transposition chart helps if your flute is in another key.

The included CD has demonstration performances by John and Karen plus background tracks for your own practice and performance.
