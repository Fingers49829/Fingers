Tunes And Techniques – Bile 'Em Cabbage Down uses the old-time tune "Bile 'Em Cabbage Down" to teach mountain dulcimer technique. It's one of five short books in Aaron O'Rourke's Tunes And Techniques series, each built around a single core tune.

Working through the tune, Aaron covers fundamental dulcimer techniques along with some advanced ones, so you come away playing better and with a complete tune under your fingers.
