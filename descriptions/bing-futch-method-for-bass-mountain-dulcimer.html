Bing Futch's Method For Bass Mountain Dulcimer is a complete instruction book for players adding the bass mountain dulcimer to their music. It starts with just enough theory to get you playing and goes on to embellishments and rhythmic variations.

The bass mountain dulcimer is typically tuned DAD, like a standard dulcimer but a full octave lower, and it fills a unique niche in solo and ensemble playing. With this book you'll learn to:

  • Play backup in any genre of music
  • Take a solo
  • Improvise, or "make it up as you go"
