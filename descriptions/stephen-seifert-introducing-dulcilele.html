Introducing Dulcilele is Stephen Seifert's instruction book for the dulcilele, a diatonic ukulele made only by Folkcraft Instruments. It has the sound of a ukulele, with some frets left out and DAD tuning like a dulcimer.

Folkcraft commissioned Stephen to write it mainly for dulcimer players moving to the dulcilele. Instruction comes first, followed by plenty of original Seifert arrangements.

83 pages.
