Build your mountain dulcimer technique with "Spotted Pony." Tunes And Techniques – Spotted Pony is one of five books in Aaron O'Rourke's Tunes And Techniques series, each using a core old-time tune to teach fundamental and some advanced dulcimer techniques.

This short book is a focused lesson with one of the best teachers and performers on the instrument.
