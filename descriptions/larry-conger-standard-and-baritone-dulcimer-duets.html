Standard And Baritone Dulcimer Duets by Larry Conger pairs a standard dulcimer in DAD with a baritone dulcimer in AEA.

On the standard dulcimer part you'll switch between DAD and DGD and use the 1.5 fret frequently, and a few songs need a capo. The set comes with a separate book for each player.

46 pages each (92 pages total).
