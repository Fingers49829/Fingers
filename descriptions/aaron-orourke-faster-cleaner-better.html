Aaron O'Rourke's Faster, Cleaner, Better! is a mountain dulcimer technique book for players who want more speed and cleaner playing. Its practice drills and exercises build speed and fluidity without strain.

Beyond the drills, Aaron goes into:

  • Fingering choices, analyzed in depth
  • Ergonomics, for playing comfortably
  • Tips for getting around the fretboard easily

Free downloadable audio lets you hear how every exercise should sound. Get the audio files from Folkcraft.
