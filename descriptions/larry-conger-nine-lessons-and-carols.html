Nine Lessons And Carols is a collection of fingerstyle Christmas arrangements for mountain dulcimer by Larry Conger, sold as an instant PDF download.

The download is a ZIP file containing:

  • The complete book as a PDF (27 pages)
  • Each song as its own PDF, so you can sort the titles (12 files)
  • An MP3 recording of each song (12 files)

Print the sheet music, or read it on your tablet or computer.
