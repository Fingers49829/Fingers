Irish Session Tunes, Volume 1 brings 36 jigs, reels and polkas from the Irish session repertoire to the mountain dulcimer, arranged by Aaron O'Rourke. These are tunes commonly played when musicians gather for an Irish session.

Along with the tab, Aaron includes a short introduction to playing in an Irish session and an explanation of Irish ornamentation.
