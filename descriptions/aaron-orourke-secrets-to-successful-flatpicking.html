Secrets To Successful Flatpicking is Aaron O'Rourke's guide to flatpicking the mountain dulcimer: technique, exercises, and great tunes to play with a pick.

Aaron pairs each exercise with practical applications, so the skills you build carry straight into real music and strengthen your flatpicking.

Free downloadable audio lets you hear how the tunes and exercises should sound. Get the audio files from Folkcraft.
