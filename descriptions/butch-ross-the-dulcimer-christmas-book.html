The Dulcimer Christmas Book features Christmas classics arranged for Appalachian dulcimer in Butch Ross's entertaining style. The arrangements are challenging but rewarding to learn, and they make a great gift of music for family holiday gatherings.

42 pages.

Songs included:

Good King Wenceslas - DAA Tuning
We Three Kings - DAD Tuning
What Child Is This? - DAD Tuning
God Rest Ye Merry Gentlemen - DAD Tuning
O Little Town Of Bethlehem - D, F Sharp, A Tuning - Composed by: Lewis H. Redner
Come All Ye Faithful - DAA Tuning
Carol Of The Bells - DAD Tuning
Hark The Herald, Angels Sing - DAA Tuning
Angels We Have Heard On High - DAA Tuning
Joy To The World - DAA Tuning
Away In A Manger - DAD Tuning
Silent Night - DAD Tuning
