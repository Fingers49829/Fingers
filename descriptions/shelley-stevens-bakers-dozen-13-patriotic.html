Baker's Dozen #13: Patriotic packs 15 patriotic arrangements for mountain dulcimer by Shelley Stevens (two more than a baker's dozen!).

  • Service songs: Army, Navy, Air Force and more
  • Classic patriotic songs by Irving Berlin, George M. Cohan and others
  • Traditional American favorites such as "America" and "Battle Hymn Of The Republic"

The arrangements are very accessible, all in DAD tuning with no capo. Five of them use the 1+ fret.

25 pages.
