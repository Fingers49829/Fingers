Join The Jam, Ukulele Edition brings Stephen Seifert's popular Join The Jam approach to the ukulele, introducing even pop-oriented ukulele players to classic old-time jam tunes.

Each tune has the melody, lyrics and chord symbols, in DGD tuning, and is in its traditional key so you can play along with most jam groups. It's compatible with the Join The Jam books for mountain dulcimer.

80 pages.
