Dulcimer-Friendly Worship, Vol. 1: The Season Of Advent arranges Advent hymn tunes for mountain dulcimer ensemble in a variety of tunings, with Steve Eulberg's historical research into the background of each tune, text and setting.

A supplement gives DAD versions of every DAA hymn, along with a suggested order for an Advent chorale service of worship.

Steve's Dulcimer-Friendly Worship series puts the church's hymns and carols within easy reach of dulcimer players.

86 pages.
